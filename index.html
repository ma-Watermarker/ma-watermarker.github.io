How It Works

Step 1: Select Your Images

Drag and drop single photos or entire folders into ma Watermarker. It auto-detects orientation for optimal handling.

Step 2: Choose Watermark Type

Opt for text or image watermarks. Pick from your repository, free patterns, or create new ones with fonts, sizes, and effects.

Step 3: Customize and Position

Fine-tune opacity, shadows, filters, rotation, and placement. Preview changes in real-time for perfection.

Step 4: Process and Export

Apply to batches, use presets if saved, rename files, and export in your preferred format. Done.
